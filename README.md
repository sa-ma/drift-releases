# Drift

### A fresh view for your Mac, every day.

Drift brings a new wallpaper to your desktop each day. Pick what you feel like seeing—nature, oceans, space, architecture, and more—or let Drift surprise you. It keeps your wallpaper in sync as you move between monitors and desktops.

**[Download Drift for Mac](https://github.com/sa-ma/drift-releases/releases/latest/download/Drift.dmg)**

Works on Macs with macOS 14 or newer, including Apple silicon and Intel models. Drift is free to use and needs no account.

## Getting started

1. Download Drift and open the downloaded file.
2. Drag **Drift** into **Applications**, then open it.
3. Choose a category in the welcome window. Drift will take it from there.

The wave icon in your menu bar lets you see the current image, try another with **Next Image**, or change categories. You can close the window and Drift will keep running. Turn on **Launch at Login** if you want it to start when you sign in.

## Getting a newer version

Quit Drift, download it again using the button above, and replace the copy in Applications. Your choices and downloaded wallpapers will still be there. Automatic app updates aren't available yet.

## Before you install

This preview has not been notarized by Apple. macOS may ask you to confirm that you want to open it in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

Drift finds images on Wallhaven. To see where a wallpaper came from, choose **View Image Source** in the app. Images belong to their respective creators.
